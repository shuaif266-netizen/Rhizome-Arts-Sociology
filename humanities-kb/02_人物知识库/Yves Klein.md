# Yves Klein

## 1960a
Buchloh places Klein among the artists who signed the Nouveau Réalisme manifesto. He discusses Klein’s Le Vide and the sale of Zones of Immaterial Pictorial Sensibility as practices that make gallery space, ownership and exchange part of the work. These are the chapter’s critical readings, not a complete account of Klein’s practice.

**Source**: *Art Since 1900*, 3rd ed., “1960a: Nouveau Réalisme,” book pp.504–508 / PDF pp.508–512.


## 1964a：Klein与新先锋派奇观

Buchloh把Klein与Beuys、Warhol并列讨论，认为战后新先锋派将文化工业的奇观机制和艺术家明星形象纳入实践。该判断属于本章作者的比较框架。来源：*Art Since 1900*，书内p.558 / PDF p.562。
