# Daniel Spoerri

## 1960a
Buchloh lists Spoerri among the manifesto signatories and discusses his table-trap practice and proposal that others produce tableaux-pièges. The chapter uses this to examine collaboration, production and exhibition.

**Source**: *Art Since 1900*, 3rd ed., “1960a: Nouveau Réalisme,” book pp.504–507 / PDF pp.508–511.
