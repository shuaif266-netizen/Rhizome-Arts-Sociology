# Jacques de la Villeglé

## 1960a
Buchloh places Villeglé among the manifesto signatories and discusses the artists’ décollage practice as a public appropriation of advertising posters. The chapter relates the works to the Lettrist/Situationist critique of spectacle while preserving the artists’ different positions.

**Source**: *Art Since 1900*, 3rd ed., “1960a: Nouveau Réalisme,” book pp.504–506 / PDF pp.508–510.
