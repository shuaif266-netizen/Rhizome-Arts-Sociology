# Raymond Hains

## 1960a
Buchloh lists Hains among the manifesto signatories and discusses his collaboration with Jacques de la Villeglé on torn advertising posters. The figure caption identifies Ach Alma Manetro (1949).

**Source**: *Art Since 1900*, 3rd ed., “1960a: Nouveau Réalisme,” book pp.504, 506 / PDF pp.508, 510, fig. 4.
