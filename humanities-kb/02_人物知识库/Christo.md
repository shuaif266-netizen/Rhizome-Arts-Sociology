# Christo

## 1960a
Buchloh names Christo among artists who joined the New Realists after the initial manifesto and discusses the public-space project Wall of Barrels, Iron Curtain (1961–62), made with Jeanne-Claude.

**Source**: *Art Since 1900*, 3rd ed., “1960a: Nouveau Réalisme,” book p.505 / PDF p.509, fig. 2.
