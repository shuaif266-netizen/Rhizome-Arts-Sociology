# Pierre Restany

## 1960a
Buchloh describes Restany as organizing artists in Yves Klein’s Paris apartment on 27 October 1960 to form Nouveau Réalisme, and as signing the group’s one-sentence manifesto with eight artists. This entry does not attempt a full intellectual biography.

**Source**: *Art Since 1900*, 3rd ed., “1960a: Nouveau Réalisme,” book p.504 / PDF p.508.
