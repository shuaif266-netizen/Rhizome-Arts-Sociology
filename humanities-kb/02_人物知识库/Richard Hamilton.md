## 1956
英国画家、拼贴作者及Independent Group成员，组织Growth and Form、Man, Machine, and Motion并参与This is Tomorrow；《Hommage à Chrysler Corp.》和《Just what is it that makes today’s homes so different, so appealing?》将汽车设计、广告、家居、性别化身体和大众媒介纳入拼贴。Foster认为其作品同时动用并质疑消费奇观。
**来源**：*Art Since 1900*, 3rd ed., book pp.448–452。
