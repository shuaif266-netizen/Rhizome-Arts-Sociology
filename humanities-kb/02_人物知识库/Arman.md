# Arman

## 1960a
Buchloh identifies Arman as a signatory of the Nouveau Réalisme manifesto and discusses Le Plein (1960), accumulations and poubelles. He interprets the accumulations through industrialized death, memory and ecological catastrophe; that is the chapter’s argument and remains open to verification against artist statements and reception.

**Source**: *Art Since 1900*, 3rd ed., “1960a: Nouveau Réalisme,” book pp.504–508 / PDF pp.508–512.
